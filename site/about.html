ONE AIRPORT, DONE PROPERLY

Departs Daily tracks cheap flights out of Charlotte Douglas and nowhere else. That narrowness is the whole point.

WHAT THIS IS

Departs Daily is a Charlotte-only flight deal board. Every morning it scans live airfare data across the routes we track out of CLT, compares what it finds against what that route normally costs, and publishes the fares that are actually below average. Then it says how far below, and shows the maths.

Most deal sites cover fifty airports and end up covering none of them well. A fare that is a steal out of Newark is often unremarkable out of Charlotte, and a site serving both cannot tell you which is which. We only have to be right about one airport, so we can be specific: which months are cheap on this route, what a normal fare looks like, whether the deal on the board today is genuinely unusual or just Tuesday.

There is also a set of city guides, one for every destination on the board. They exist because the flight is the easy part. Where to sleep, what is worth booking, and which week to avoid is the part people actually get wrong.

WHO RUNS IT

Departs Daily is an independent, one-person operation based in Charlotte, published by Ghost Pepper Joe, LLC. It is not owned by an airline, an online travel agency, or a booking engine, and nobody pays to appear on the board. The daily post also runs on Instagram as @cltdeparts.

Being small is why the board can be blunt. There is no partner to keep happy, so when a fare is mediocre we leave it off, and when a popular thing in a city is not worth your money the guide says so.

HOW THE FARES ARE FOUND

1 · Scan. Several times a day an automated pipeline sweeps live airfare search data across every route we track from CLT. These are real fares surfaced in real searches, not invented sale prices.
2 · Compare. Each candidate fare is measured against that specific route's typical round-trip cost. For domestic routes the baseline is official U.S. Department of Transportation data from the Bureau of Transportation Statistics Consumer Airfare Report, all four quarters of 2025, with each-way fares doubled to a round-trip equivalent. International baselines are our own estimates and are labelled as estimates everywhere they appear.
3 · Publish. The "percent below average" badge on every row is calculated from those two numbers at render time. It is never typed by hand, which means a claim on the board cannot overstate the saving even if we wanted it to.
4 · Expire. Cheap fares do not last. When the board's week runs out it says so plainly rather than showing you a stale price.

WHAT WE WON'T DO

We do not sell travel, hold your booking, or take your payment. Every link goes to an airline or a booking site, and their terms govern what happens next. The price at checkout is the only price that counts, and we say so on every page for a reason: fares move between the moment we find one and the moment you click.

We do not publish fares we have not measured, and we do not dress up an ordinary price as a deal to fill a slot. A thin day on the board is an honest thin day.

The site carries affiliate links and display advertising. If you book through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That relationship never decides what makes the board, because the board is chosen by price against baseline before any link is attached. The full detail is on the affiliate disclosure page.

GET IN TOUCH

Want a route tracked, a city added, or a correction made? The suggestion box on the deal board reaches us directly, and corrections get priority over feature requests. If something on this site is wrong, we would rather hear it from you than leave it up.
