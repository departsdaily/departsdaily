HOW WE MAKE MONEY

Plainly, and in full — because you should know exactly what happens when you click.

Departs Daily is reader-supported. Some links on this website are affiliate links. That means if you click a link and then book a flight, reserve a hotel, buy a tour, or purchase a product, we may earn a commission from the company you booked with. This never costs you anything extra — the price you pay is the same whether you use our link or go direct.

We participate in affiliate programs that may include, among others: travel booking networks (such as Travelpayouts and its partner brands), online travel agencies and their partner programs (such as the Expedia Group affiliate program and Booking.com's partner program), tour and activity marketplaces (such as Viator and GetYourGuide), and the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

WHAT WE PROMISE

  • Deals come first. Fares make the board because our data says they're genuinely below typical prices — never because of a commission.
  • Recommendations are real. Hotels and tours in our city guides are picked on merit. If a great option has no affiliate program, we recommend it anyway.
  • Prices change fast. Fares and rates shown are examples spotted at publication time and are not guaranteed. Always confirm the final price on the booking site before paying.
