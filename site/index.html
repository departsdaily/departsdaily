How the numbers work

1 · Scan. Several times a day, our system sweeps live airfare search data across every route we track from the selected airport — real fares found in real searches.
2 · Compare. Each fare is measured against the route's average price — official DOT/BTS government data on domestic routes, clearly-labeled estimates on international ones. No baseline, no post.
3 · Rank. Only the top savings make the board — and nothing repeats within 6 days, so the board is always fresh.
