How the numbers work

1 · Scan. Every hour, around the clock, our system sweeps live airfare search data across all 30 tracked routes from CLT — real fares found in real searches.
2 · Compare. Each fare is measured against the route's average price — official DOT/BTS government data on domestic routes, clearly-labeled estimates on international ones. No baseline, no post.
3 · Rank. Only the top savings make the board — and nothing repeats within 6 days, so the board is always fresh.

Where these numbers come from: for domestic routes, the annual average is official U.S. DOT/BTS data — the Consumer Airfare Report's average fare for this city-pair across all four quarters of 2025 (each-way fares doubled to a round-trip equivalent; includes all cabins, taxes and fees). The month-by-month bars on every route are an estimated seasonal pattern scaled to the annual average — a booking guide, not a price quote.
