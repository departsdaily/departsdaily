DO IT CHEAP — THE PLAYBOOK

Fly Sun–Thu. Weekend fares and hotel rates are 2–3× midweek.
Count the resort fee. A "$35 room" with a $50 resort fee is an $85 room.
Eat one block off-Strip. Chinatown on Spring Mountain Road has the best food in the city at a third of Strip prices.
Free shows first. Bellagio fountains, the Sphere exterior, Fremont Street — schedule the free spectacle, then decide what's worth paying for.
