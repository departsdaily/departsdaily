■ CITY GUIDE — CUN · INTERNATIONAL

CANCÚN, DONE RIGHT

Turquoise water two hours from most U.S. hubs. Enjoy the beach, then escape the resort at least twice — once for ruins or a cenote, once for a boat day.

CHEAP MONTHS
DEC–APR
Dry and sunny. May–Oct is hotter, wetter, and much cheaper; hurricane season peaks Sep–Oct.
