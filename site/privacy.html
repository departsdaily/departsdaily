YOUR PRIVACY

What we collect, what we don't, and who else is involved.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Email address — only if you sign up for our deal emails. We use it to send you the deal board and nothing else. Unsubscribe anytime with one click.

Basic analytics — like most websites, we may use analytics tools to understand which pages and deals people find useful (pages visited, approximate location, device type). This data is aggregated and doesn't personally identify you.

COOKIES & THIRD PARTIES

When you click a booking or product link, you leave our site and land on a partner's site (an airline search engine, hotel booking site, tour marketplace, or Amazon). Those partners may set cookies to attribute your booking to us — that's how affiliate commissions work — and their own privacy policies apply on their sites. If we display advertising (such as Google AdSense), the ad provider may use cookies to show relevant ads; you can manage ad personalization in your browser and through the provider's settings.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't see or store any payment details — all bookings happen on our partners' sites.
  • We don't send spam. Deal emails only, and only if you asked.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can request access to, or deletion of, any personal data we hold about you (realistically: your email address) by writing to departshq@gmail.com. We'll handle it promptly.
